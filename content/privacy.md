## Free tools

The student budget calculator runs in your browser. It does not connect to your bank, transmit the numbers you enter, or save them to a server. Refreshing the page resets the form. Downloads are generated locally or served as static files. Do not enter account numbers or other sensitive information.

## Website hosting and external links

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting infrastructure can process technical request information under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Links to product providers and other websites take you to services with their own terms and privacy policies.

This makeover does not add advertising trackers, bank connections, or a newsletter signup. Search runs against the site's article index in your browser.
