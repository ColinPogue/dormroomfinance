Dorm Room Finance is an independent educational project by Colin Pogue. The goal is to help students understand financial decisions, including the limitations of the information presented.

## Sources and review dates

Refreshed guides link to the official providers, regulators, or government resources used in the update. Product availability, fees, rewards, eligibility, and tax or repayment rules can change. A review date records when a guide was updated; it is not a guarantee that every term remains current. The provider's current disclosures control.

We prioritize eligibility, cost, risk, and usefulness to students over signup bonuses. Comparisons describe a selected set of options, not the entire market. We do not promise approval, a particular credit score, investment returns, or specific earnings.

## AI assistance and personal experience

AI assists with drafting, editing, research organization, and website development. The older automated publishing process could generate unverified first-person stories and outdated product details. Articles from that process carry a visible archive notice until they receive a substantive update.

Refreshed guides use clearly labeled hypothetical examples. They do not claim Colin personally used a product or experienced an event unless that experience has been confirmed. New articles are researched, generated, checked, and published automatically using AI. They are not individually reviewed by Colin before publication. Automated checks can miss errors; confirm important financial details with the linked official sources. No human approval is needed for the publishing schedule.

## Corrections and changes

Send the page URL, the claim in question, and a supporting source to [cpogue208@gmail.com](mailto:cpogue208@gmail.com). Substantive corrections to refreshed guides are described in their update notes. Publication dates are retained; update dates change when the content changes meaningfully.

The November 2026 makeover introduces these standards. An archive label means a full current-facts review is still pending, not that every statement in the article is wrong.

## Money and independence

This site is not a lender, broker, investment adviser, or comparison service covering all available products. Product links do not imply a commercial relationship. Where a link earns compensation or a relationship affects a recommendation, it must be disclosed near that link. A configured referral link or placeholder is not proof of an active partnership.

## Use the information appropriately

This is general education for a primarily US audience. Your circumstances, location, age, income, and eligibility matter. Seek qualified professional help when a decision calls for personalized advice.
