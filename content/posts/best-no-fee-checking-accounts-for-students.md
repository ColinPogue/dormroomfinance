No monthly maintenance fee does not mean no possible charges. Read the account's fee schedule before treating a checking account as free.

## Look for the fees you could actually trigger

Check monthly maintenance fees, any minimum balance or direct-deposit waiver, ATM charges, cash deposit restrictions, overdraft treatment, replacement cards, and outgoing transfer fees. Consider both the bank's fee and any charge by an outside ATM operator.

## Check whether student status changes the terms

A student waiver may expire at a particular age, after a period of time, or when enrollment ends. Ask what happens next and whether you need to take action. Keep the answer with the account agreement.

## Match access to your routine

A fully online account may suit someone who rarely handles cash. A local branch or a useful ATM network may matter more if you deposit cash earnings or move between campus and home. Compare the actual services rather than the label.

Before switching, confirm the new account is open and usable, update direct deposit and bills, and keep enough money available while changes settle.
