How to use the result

A positive balance is money you have not assigned yet. A negative balance means planned outgoings exceed income. Savings is a planned allocation, not an expense paid to someone else. Check real payment dates separately, especially when income is seasonal.

Do not double-count credit card purchases: include current purchases in their spending categories, and use debt payments for existing debt you are repaying. If a parent or scholarship pays a bill directly, omit both that bill and the matching support from this cash budget.
